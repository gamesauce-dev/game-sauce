Quick launch cache

A platform with quick launch ticked keeps its games here after the emulator closes, so the next launch of the same game needs no download and no unpacking. If the game changes on the server, the kept copy is replaced. The games played longest ago are removed when a folder passes its limit. Saves an emulator wrote next to a game are copied to ~/.gamesauce/rescued before it is removed.
