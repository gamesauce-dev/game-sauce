# Icons for the Modern look

Small SVG pictures drawn on a 24-pixel grid, used by the Modern look. Where they come from:

## From IconOven (114 of the 151)

Most of these icons are from **IconOven** by **Appic Marketing**, under the **MIT licence** (https://iconoven.com/icons; also listed on https://icon-sets.iconify.design/iconoven/). Iconify lists it as "no attribution required, commercial use allowed"; it is credited here anyway.

- Exactly as published in IconOven (shapes identical): 112 files.
- Combined with new shapes: add-collection.svg, game-add.svg.
- Some of the others have been adjusted by hand (colour, size or a shape changed).
